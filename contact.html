Common Questions

FAQs

Do I need to book an appointment? +

No — just turn up during opening hours and we'll help you. No booking, no waiting lists. If you have a specific tyre size in mind, feel free to ring ahead to check we have it in stock.

Do you accept card payment? +

We currently accept cash only. Please bring the right amount. There are ATMs nearby. We'll always tell you the price before starting any work so there are no surprises.

How long does tyre fitting take? +

Most jobs are done in 15–30 minutes. Puncture repairs typically take around 20 minutes. Single tyre fitting takes about 15 minutes. We work quickly so you're not waiting around.

Are part-worn tyres safe? +

Yes — we only stock part-worn tyres that are safe and have legal tread depth. We check every tyre before it goes out. We'll also show you the tyre before fitting so you can see its condition.

What if you don't have my tyre size in stock? +

Ring ahead and we'll check for you. If we don't have your size, we can often source it quickly. Call us on 01942 000 000 and we'll let you know.
